# RabPDF Windows privacy information

Last updated: 2026-10-06. Applies to Windows version 1.5.6.

PDF, image, QR decoding and bundled AI image-upscaling operations run locally.
RabPDF does not operate a document-upload server, require a RabPDF account,
display ads, or include developer-run analytics in this Windows build.
Windows tools are free and do not request payment-card details.

The app checks GitHub for available releases. GitHub receives normal connection
metadata, such as the network IP address; PDFs and images are not included in
these requests. Update installation is your choice. Sponsorship enquiries open
your email client, and linked websites follow their own privacy policies.

The app accesses files you select or drop and writes results to locations you
choose. Processing can use temporary files and memory. Sensitive document or
QR contents are not automatically sent to the developer. Do not treat local
processing as encryption, secure deletion, or a guarantee against breaches.

Your language preference is stored locally under
%LOCALAPPDATA%\RabPDF\settings.json. No other identifier is required to use
the Windows tools. Outputs remain on your computer until you move or delete
them. Cloud-synced folders can upload exported files through their provider.

QR codes can contain personal data, contact details or Wi-Fi passwords. Anyone
with the generated image can read that content. The QR reader displays decoded
contents and does not automatically open links. Opening a confirmed web link,
the Instagram page or an email button uses your external browser/email app;
those services have their own data practices. Windows and security software
may also process diagnostic or security information independently of RabPDF.

If you contact support, the mailbox service and recipient receive your email
address, message and any attachments you send. Send only necessary information.
Do not send private documents unless you deliberately choose to do so, and do
not post them in public GitHub issues. Ask by email about support-message
retention or deletion; legal retention obligations may apply.
